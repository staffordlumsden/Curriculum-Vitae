Executive Profile

Award-winning educational designer, applied linguist and AI-in-education leader with nearly two decades of teaching, curriculum, online learning and professional education experience. I specialise in turning complex ideas into clear, reusable learning systems for adult and professional audiences, with particular expertise in responsible generative AI, assessment, multimodal learning design and capability development. At Sydney Law School I lead educational design across curriculum, assessment and AI integration, while building practical AI-assisted workflows, openly reusable resources and professional-learning materials that others can adapt and teach without ongoing oversight. My practice combines evidence-based pedagogy, strong general-audience writing, rapid prototyping, technical experimentation and data-informed evaluation.
