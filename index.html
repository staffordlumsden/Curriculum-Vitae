About

Evidence-led design for complex educational systems.

Stafford works at the intersection of pedagogy, curriculum governance, legal education, applied linguistics, digital learning and organisational capability.

As the in-house Educational Designer at the University of Sydney Law School, Stafford supports and leads work across curriculum design, assessment reform, academic quality assurance, policy implementation, educator capability and responsible AI integration in LLB, JD, postgraduate and professional law contexts.

His specialist practice includes role and capability mapping, learning-outcome architecture, constructive alignment, adult learning, authentic assessment, program evaluation and evidence-based review. His work translates educational research, policy and regulatory requirements into approaches that are academically defensible and operationally workable.

Stafford holds a PhD in Applied Linguistics and researches multimodal social semiotics, online learning environments, educator professional development, legal education and human–AI interaction. His career spans Australia, New Zealand and South Korea.
